# Plans and billing

## Plans

Hanami Cloud has three plans. Free includes 5 GB of storage at no cost. Plus includes 200 GB and costs $4 per month. Pro includes 2 TB and costs $12 per month.

## Annual billing

Choosing annual billing saves 20 percent compared with paying monthly. You can switch between monthly and annual billing from the Billing page.

## Refunds

Annual plans can be refunded within 14 days of the first purchase. Monthly plans are not refundable. Refund requests are made by contacting support.

## Payment methods and invoices

We accept credit cards and PayPal. An invoice is emailed to you on the day your plan renews.

## Failed payments

If a payment fails, we retry it 3 times over 7 days. If all retries fail, the account is downgraded to the Free plan. Files above the 5 GB Free limit stay readable, but you cannot upload new files until you are under the limit again.
