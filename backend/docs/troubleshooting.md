# Troubleshooting and support

## Sync is stuck

If sync seems stuck, first pause and resume sync from the app menu. Then check that your device has enough free disk space. If it is still stuck, restart the app.

## Error codes

Error H-401 means your login has expired. Sign in again to fix it. Error H-507 means your storage is full. Delete files or upgrade your plan to continue uploading.

## Contacting support
